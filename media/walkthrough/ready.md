### Sensible defaults out of the box

You don't need to configure anything to start watching videos:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Default viewer** | Enabled | Clicking MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM, AVI, and 15+ other formats opens them right in VS Code. |
| **Decoder** | Auto | Uses your GPU automatically for H.264 and VP8, and the built-in decoder for everything else. |
| **Frame sizing** | Fit to view | Scales large videos to your panel size so playback stays smooth; grows automatically when you zoom or go fullscreen. |
| **Live transcode** | Auto | Keeps playback smooth on Remote-SSH and prevents local 4K60 stalls automatically. |

Want VS Code's built-in preview back for MP4/WebM? Run **Video Preview: Use VS Code's Preview for MP4/WebM** anytime.
