### Clickable frame & compare links for AI agents

Teach **Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or Copilot** to output clickable `vscode://` links whenever they generate, transcode, or compare videos:

```text
vscode://gordon-vidal.video-preview-enhanced/open?frame=142&file=/abs/path/render.mp4
vscode://gordon-vidal.video-preview-enhanced/compare?frame=142&mode=split&file=/a.mp4&file=/b.mp4
```

Clicking a link in chat or the integrated terminal jumps straight to that exact frame or opens a synchronized side-by-side comparison in VS Code.

Click **Copy AI Agent Instructions** on the left and paste the snippet into your `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, or `.cursorrules`.
