### Optional portable ffmpeg (~80 MB, no admin rights)

Common videos (**H.264** and **VP8**) already play on your graphics card with nothing installed.

Setting up **ffmpeg** unlocks full-speed hardware playback for heavy formats:

- **4K & 60 fps** video
- **HDR** (HDR10, HLG, Dolby Vision profile 8)
- **HEVC (H.265), AV1, VP9, ProRes, DNxHR, MXF**

**How it works:**
- Downloads a verified portable build (LGPL) into VS Code's own extension folder — nothing is installed system-wide, or you can point to an `ffmpeg` you already have.
- Nothing runs in the background after setup.
